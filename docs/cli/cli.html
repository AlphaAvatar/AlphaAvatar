AlphaAvatar CLI output

What a terminal of each width shows once the command ends, rendered from its recording with the escape codes kept as colors (scripts/docs/cli_preview.py). Setup, config, and Doctor declined or with --no-metrics ran on a clean Ubuntu 24.04 root with the Linux package of this build. Doctor's measured runs used the offline test doubles of its tests, marked 离线测试替身: their services and model files are stand-ins and their timings are the doubles', in rounds of their own. No panel here holds a real service's figures: those are recorded apart, in the acceptance log. Every figure ends at the Host's output; LiveKit's transport, a client's playback and the devices are measured separately. Lines wider than a panel are diagnostics and commands, which print whole.
